<h2>First decision: which link is missing</h2><p>Check known faults and repair cost: some bodies have shutter/mainboard/slot issues. Used is worth the risk below 60% of new; below 50% usually hides something. For lenses, check fungus, scratches, and AF motor; for bodies, shutter, sensor, ports, and slots.</p><p><strong>Fits:</strong></p><ul><li>Limited budget, previous flagship</li><li>Test shoot/in-person OK</li><li>Repair channel available</li></ul><p><strong>Does not fit:</strong></p><ul><li>Need new warranty</li><li>No test/return</li><li>Risky model/grey import</li></ul><h2>Test protocol: reproducible, no invented scores</h2><p>Meet with a laptop and card: shoot a white wall for dead pixels, 30-second exposure for hot noise, video for rolling shutter/AF, test HDMI/USB/slots, and check shutter count and serial.</p><div class="table-scroll"><table class="data-table"><thead><tr><th>Check</th><th>Pass line</th><th>Reference/log</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>快门</td><td>低于标称寿命 50%</td><td>快门数</td></tr><tr><td>传感器</td><td>白墙无坏点，长曝无异常热噪</td><td>白墙/30 秒</td></tr><tr><td>接口</td><td>HDMI/USB/卡槽逐个正常</td><td>现场测试</td></tr><tr><td>维修</td><td>序列号可查，有维修记录</td><td>官方/第三方</td></tr><tr><td>价格</td><td>新机 60–75% 以下</td><td>行情对比</td></tr></tbody></table></div><h2>Price bands: total cost of ownership first</h2><p>Look beyond the sticker: cards, batteries, adapters, gimbal, stands, software subscriptions, and repair. Bands describe the minimum kit for a real project type, not toy pricing.</p><div class="table-scroll"><table class="data-table"><thead><tr><th>Tier</th><th>Reference</th><th>Includes</th><th>Watch</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Entry</td><td>¥1,000–3,000</td><td>Previous APS-C/lens</td><td>Shutter/sensor risk</td></tr><tr><td>Main</td><td>¥3,000–8,000</td><td>Previous full-frame/pro lens</td><td>Service/accessories</td></tr><tr><td>Advanced</td><td>¥8,000–20,000</td><td>Used cinema/pro lens</td><td>Test/maintenance</td></tr></tbody></table></div><p>Prices are China mainland channel references as of November 2026; tax and quotes vary. Not buying advice.</p><h2>Alternatives and upgrades: do not add money on the wrong axis</h2><ul><li>Rent a month first</li><li>Buy official refurbished</li><li>New entry instead of old flagship</li></ul><h2>Buying steps: bring footage, not spec sheets</h2><p>Run these in order before paying. If one fails, do not tell yourself post will fix it.</p><ol><li>Check known faults/repair cost</li><li>White wall/long exposure</li><li>Test each port/slot</li><li>Check shutter/serial</li><li>Write return/warranty terms</li></ol><h2>Check</h2><ul><li>Shutter under 50% life.</li><li>Sensor has no dead pixels/scratches/fungus.</li><li>All ports/slots work.</li><li>Serial is checkable.</li><li>Price below 60–75% of new.</li><li>Test/return terms exist.</li></ul>
