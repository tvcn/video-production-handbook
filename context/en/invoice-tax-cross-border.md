<h2>Scope</h2><p>This page covers general billing/invoicing for China mainland producers serving cross-border film/ad projects; it is not tax or legal advice. Rates, exemption/zero-rating, withholding, and permanent establishment depend on facts; a CPA/tax adviser must review.</p><h2>Key terms: five decisions</h2><p><strong>1. Entity</strong>: domestic producer or offshore/HK entity decides invoicing and tax. <strong>2. Place of service</strong>: shoot/post inside or outside China; offshore may qualify for exemption/zero-rating with filing and evidence. <strong>3. PE</strong>: a long-term shoot/fixed place may create a permanent establishment. <strong>4. Invoice</strong>: VAT special/general, proforma, and commercial invoices serve different purposes; a proforma does not replace a tax invoice. <strong>5. Withholding</strong>: an offshore client paying a domestic producer may withhold VAT/income tax; state tax-inclusive/exclusive and who bears it.</p><h2>Process: ask tax before quoting</h2><p>Make a one-page fact sheet: client location, contracting entity, place of service, crew/gear export, currency, tax inclusive, withholding, PE. Have tax confirm invoice type, rate/exemption filing, and contract terms. Include tax, transfer fees, and withholding in the quote; do not quote on “the client says no tax.”</p><h2>Common disputes: four blockers</h2><p><strong>“Proforma first”</strong>: fine for quote/deposit, not for tax accounting. <strong>“Offshore service is exempt”</strong>: needs contract, travel records, deliverables, and payment evidence; not automatic. <strong>“Client withholds, we earn less”</strong>: if the contract is silent, it comes out of the producer’s margin. <strong>“HK company, no tax”</strong>: management and place of service matter; changing the payee is not enough.</p><h2>Template: six cross-border clauses</h2><p>Scope and place of delivery; entity and bank account; currency and FX; tax-inclusive/exclusive and withholding; invoice type and timing; tax/customs/visa/ATA responsibility. Attach the fact sheet and tax confirmation email.</p><h2>Check</h2><ul><li>Entity and place of service are written.</li><li>Invoice type/rate confirmed.</li><li>Tax inclusive/exclusive and withholding are written.</li><li>Transfer fees are in the quote.</li><li>Exemption/zero-rating has evidence.</li><li>PE/visa/ATA assessed.</li></ul>
