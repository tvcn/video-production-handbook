<h2>On set</h2>
<p>Early Technicolor split light into three colors at once. Watch red clothing and skin; color was sold as spectacle.</p>
<h2>Do this</h2>
<p>Technicolor was not natural color; it made color a spectacle and sold tickets on “redder red.”</p><ol><li>See how a beam splitter records three colors at once.</li><li>Compare a red garment with skin tone.</li><li>Decide whether color serves spectacle or realism.</li></ol>
<h2>Avoid</h2>
<p>Do not treat Technicolor as faithful reproduction; its red is designed.</p>
<h2>Check</h2>
<ul><li>The three-strip beam-splitter process is described.</li><li>Red garment and skin tone are sampled separately.</li><li>A shot shows whether color serves spectacle or realism.</li></ul>
