<h2>On set</h2>
<p>From filters and restoration to keying and generation, the key change is control. A prompt is not a director.</p>
<h2>Do this</h2>
<p>AI video did not start with generation; filters, restoration, keying, and face swap each trained automatic image modification.</p><ol><li>Build a timeline: filters, restoration, keying, face swap, generation.</li><li>Mark what a human could still control at each step.</li><li>See what a prompt can and cannot control.</li></ol>
<h2>Avoid</h2>
<p>Do not treat a prompt as a director; it offers probability, not blocking.</p>
<h2>Check</h2>
<ul><li>The timeline runs from filters and restoration through keying and face swap to generation.</li><li>Each step names the human control that remained.</li><li>One thing prompts cannot control is named, such as exact blocking or continuity.</li></ul>
