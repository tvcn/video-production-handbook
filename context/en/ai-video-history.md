<p>AI video did not start with generation. It moved through filters, style transfer, upscaling, matting and frame interpolation before text-to-video. Each step turned post repair into pre-generation, and pushed authorship, rights and evidence to the front.</p>

<section><h2 id="section-2">How to use it now</h2><ul><li>On an AI project, log model, version, prompt, references and output location as carefully as live-action footage.</li><li>Use AI for inserts, backgrounds, pickups and concept frames; do not replace a hero face, news scene or testimony.</li><li>When studying AI history, read algorithms, compute, copyright and platform policy together, not only demos.</li></ul><h2 id="section-3">Do not misread</h2><p>Do not treat “generated” as “sourceless.” Assets without a rights chain and version record do not enter a paid delivery.</p></section><section><h2 id="section-4">On set</h2><p>From filters and restoration to keying and generation, the key change is control. A prompt is not a director.</p></section><section><h2 id="section-5">Do this</h2><p>AI video did not start with generation; filters, restoration, keying, and face swap each trained automatic image modification.</p><ol><li>Build a timeline: filters, restoration, keying, face swap, generation.</li><li>Mark what a human could still control at each step.</li><li>See what a prompt can and cannot control.</li></ol></section><section><h2 id="section-6">Avoid</h2><p>Do not treat a prompt as a director; it offers probability, not blocking.</p></section><section><p><strong>Use it today</strong></p><p>Put the history back into a job: archive, restoration, teaching or a short film. Ask what it solved, then what it still cannot do.</p><ul><li>Find one contemporary work and mark one old limit it inherited.</li><li>Find one condition it no longer needs and say why.</li><li>Write one thing it still cannot do today.</li></ul></section><section><h2>Source boundary</h2><p>This is interpretation, not a primary archive. Check years, people and statutes against the original links in <a href="../../about/">SOURCES.md</a>. A secondhand summary is a lead, not evidence.</p></section>
