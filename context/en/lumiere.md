<h2>On set</h2>
<p>Arrival of a Train used a fixed camera and diagonal depth. The audience feared the train coming at the lens, not 3D.</p>
<h2>Do this</h2>
<p>The Lumières projected a train arriving, and audiences were frightened by something fake for the first time.</p><ol><li>Lock the camera and let the train approach on a diagonal into depth.</li><li>Do not cut; hold one long take.</li><li>Note that the fear comes from motion toward the lens, not 3D.</li></ol>
<h2>Avoid</h2>
<p>Do not attribute the reaction to 3D; it is a train coming toward the camera.</p>
<h2>Check</h2>
<ul><li>The camera is locked and the train enters on a diagonal into depth.</li><li>No cut occurs; one long take carries the shot.</li><li>The fear comes from motion toward the lens, not stereoscopy.</li></ul>
