<h2>On set</h2>
<p>The Horse in Motion used 24 cameras along the track, each triggered once. Watch the grid and each frame position; it was not one continuous camera.</p>
<h2>Do this</h2>
<p>Muybridge used a row of cameras to split a horse's motion, showing what the eye was too slow to see.</p><ol><li>Place 24 cameras along the track, each firing once.</li><li>Put a grid behind the subject to compare positions frame by frame.</li><li>View the 24 frames in order, not as one continuous camera.</li></ol>
<h2>Avoid</h2>
<p>Do not treat The Horse in Motion as one continuous camera; it is 24 cameras each exposing once.</p>
<h2>Check</h2>
<ul><li>Twenty-four cameras line the track and each fires once.</li><li>The background grid aligns leg positions frame by frame.</li><li>Sequential playback identifies when all four hooves leave the ground.</li></ul>
