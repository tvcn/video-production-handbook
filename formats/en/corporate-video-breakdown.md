<h2>Sample note: not a real client</h2><p>Composite sample: a cold-chain warehouse company. Not one real project; people, numbers, and locations are illustrative.</p><p>The point is not to memorize shots but to see what makes each beat work. For a client film, replace every number, brand, and person with your own footage and evidence.</p><h2>Beat by beat: five columns</h2><p>Read time, then beat, then whether picture and sound serve the same job. If one column disagrees, the beat becomes “pretty but useless.”</p><div class="table-scroll"><table class="data-table"><thead><tr><th>Time</th><th>Beat</th><th>Picture</th><th>Sound</th><th>Job</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>00:00–00:08</td><td>Problem</td><td>Warehouse door, thermometer</td><td>Ambience + one line</td><td>Show the loss</td></tr><tr><td>00:08–00:25</td><td>Person</td><td>Night supervisor checks shelves</td><td>Live sound</td><td>One person owns it</td></tr><tr><td>00:25–00:45</td><td>Old way</td><td>Paper log, repeated calls</td><td>Keyboard/phone</td><td>Feel the cost</td></tr><tr><td>00:45–01:10</td><td>Intervention</td><td>System screen + one action</td><td>Notification</td><td>Show one change</td></tr><tr><td>01:10–01:35</td><td>Result</td><td>Temperature curve, signature</td><td>Quiet</td><td>Close on evidence</td></tr><tr><td>01:35–01:45</td><td>Next</td><td>Supervisor leaves</td><td>Ambience</td><td>No slogan</td></tr></tbody></table></div><h2>Picture and sound: what to copy, what not</h2><p><strong>Copy</strong>: before/after of one action, hand close-ups, ambience cuts, locked-off repetition. <strong>Do not copy</strong>: slow motion for emotion, music covering location sound, illustrative numbers as results. Borrow shots, not evidence.</p><h2>Three reusable moves</h2><p>1. Shoot the same action twice—failure and success—and cut them together; the viewer compares. 2. Every 15 seconds, give a hand/object close-up to ground abstract information. 3. Do not summarize at the end; leave an action or a question as a door to the next piece.</p><h2>Failure and check: breakdown is not copying</h2><p>The common failure is “the shots look similar, but the proof is fake.” Ask three questions: what happens if this beat is removed? Does the sound stand alone? Is the result checkable? If two answers fail, re-shoot or re-cut.</p><h2>Check</h2><ul><li>Labeled composite, not a real client.</li><li>Picture and sound serve the same job per beat.</li><li>At least one before/after is checkable.</li><li>Music does not cover key location sound.</li><li>Ending leaves an action/question, no summary.</li><li>Structure survives removing any one beat.</li></ul>
