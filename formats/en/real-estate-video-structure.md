<h2>First decision: when to use it</h2><p>Real-estate video is not a wide-angle building tour. Start with the life problem of who lives here, then prove it with space; every shot answers one living question.</p><p>Use it when there is one clear subject, one action, and a visible change in the final film. Do not use it when there is only a slogan, no person, and no process to shoot; go back to the brief before rolling.</p><h2>Six beats: one job per beat</h2><p>Write the six beats as six sentences, each answering “what does the viewer know now.” Shoot in order, but cut out of order; do not add empty shots to fill the structure.</p><ol><li>Doorway problem</li><li>Spatial route</li><li>One function</li><li>One detail</li><li>Light/time</li><li>Leaving</li></ol><h2>How to shoot it: six tasks</h2><p>At least one primary shot per beat; do not shoot everything in one framing and rely on jump cuts. Shoot irreplaceable action first—hands, face, machine—then pickups.</p><ul><li>Doorway: keys/shoes/parcel</li><li>Route: door to window in one move</li><li>Function: one action proves it (cook/storage)</li><li>Detail: hand/material/edge</li><li>Light: same room morning/evening</li><li>Leaving: lights off/door closes, one question left</li></ul><h2>Common failures: three ways it collapses</h2><p><strong>1. Logo/drone first</strong>: the viewer does not know whose story this is. Replace beat one with a problem or action. <strong>2. Narration in every beat</strong>: the images carry no information. Let one action speak. <strong>3. Unverifiable result</strong>: “more efficient” with no before/after or third party. Shoot something checkable.</p><h2>Cost and check: can it be shot in a day</h2><p>Typically one shoot day plus two to three post days: half a day for primary shots, half for pickups; cut structure first, then sound. Check only three things: six beats complete, one primary shot per beat, result checkable. Do not accept “it feels premium.”</p><h2>Check</h2><ul><li>Each of six beats is one clear sentence.</li><li>Beat one is a problem/action, not a logo.</li><li>At least one primary shot per beat.</li><li>Result has before/after or third party.</li><li>At least one irreplaceable action captured.</li><li>Six beats survive the rough cut.</li></ul>
