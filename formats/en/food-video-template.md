<h2>When to use this sheet</h2><p>Fill it 24 hours before rolling and send one copy each to director, camera, sound, and producer. It does not replace the storyboard; it compresses it into “what must be shot today.” When the client changes scope, change the sheet before the budget.</p><h2>The sheet: five columns plus a pass line</h2><p>One beat per row. Picture is what the viewer sees, not “premium”; sound is what they hear; pass line is what must be captured to pass.</p><div class="table-scroll"><table class="data-table"><thead><tr><th>Beat</th><th>Picture</th><th>Sound</th><th>Duration</th><th>Pass line</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Ingredient</td><td>Macro texture</td><td>Knife/water/oil</td><td>5–8 sec</td><td>Ingredient readable</td></tr><tr><td>Hands</td><td>Hands enter</td><td>Action sounds</td><td>10–15 sec</td><td>Hands are working</td></tr><tr><td>Heat</td><td>Pan/steam</td><td>Boil</td><td>10–15 sec</td><td>Heat visible</td></tr><tr><td>Change</td><td>Same angle before/after</td><td>Sound shifts</td><td>10–15 sec</td><td>One change</td></tr><tr><td>Bite</td><td>Face/mouth</td><td>Chewing</td><td>10–15 sec</td><td>No exaggeration</td></tr><tr><td>Aftertaste</td><td>Empty plate/hand</td><td>Ambience</td><td>5–8 sec</td><td>No “delicious”</td></tr></tbody></table></div><h2>Filling example: hardest first</h2><p>Fill the beats that need people, weather, or permits first; pickups last. Durations are ceilings, not targets. Stop when the pass line is met; do not repeat to fill time.</p><h2>On set: how to run the sheet</h2><p>Five minutes before rolling, read the sheet: which rows today, what pass line, who owns it. After each row, tick it and note the actual take. Photograph the sheet at wrap and archive it with the footage.</p><h2>Check: a filled sheet is not a finished film</h2><p>At wrap, check only: every pass line has footage, sound has an independent backup, the hardest beat has at least two takes. If one is missing, pick it up next day, not in post.</p><h2>Check</h2><ul><li>One row per beat, no merging.</li><li>Picture is what is seen, not adjectives.</li><li>Sound is what is heard, including ambience.</li><li>Pass line is judgeable, not “feels right.”</li><li>Hardest beat shot first.</li><li>Sheet archived with footage at wrap.</li></ul>
