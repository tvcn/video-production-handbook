<h2>Sample note: not a real client</h2><p>Composite sample: an amateur runner preparing for a city half-marathon. Not one real project; data, locations, and people are composite.</p><p>The point is not to memorize shots but to see what makes each beat work. For a client film, replace every number, brand, and person with your own footage and evidence.</p><h2>Beat by beat: five columns</h2><p>Read time, then beat, then whether picture and sound serve the same job. If one column disagrees, the beat becomes “pretty but useless.”</p><div class="table-scroll"><table class="data-table"><thead><tr><th>Time</th><th>Beat</th><th>Picture</th><th>Sound</th><th>Job</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>00:00–00:10</td><td>Before</td><td>Laces, breath, start line</td><td>Heartbeat/ambience</td><td>Body first, no slogan</td></tr><tr><td>00:10–00:30</td><td>Failure</td><td>Stumble/pacing collapse</td><td>Shoe friction</td><td>Make failure concrete</td></tr><tr><td>00:30–00:55</td><td>Training</td><td>Same road three times</td><td>Breath/footsteps</td><td>Make repetition visible</td></tr><tr><td>00:55–01:15</td><td>Breaking point</td><td>Stop, hands on knees</td><td>Ambience drops</td><td>Do not rush the answer</td></tr><tr><td>01:15–01:35</td><td>Result</td><td>Finish/timer</td><td>Crowd</td><td>One number only</td></tr><tr><td>01:35–01:45</td><td>Aftermath</td><td>Sit, take off shoes</td><td>Breath</td><td>No summary</td></tr></tbody></table></div><h2>Picture and sound: what to copy, what not</h2><p><strong>Copy</strong>: before/after of one action, hand close-ups, ambience cuts, locked-off repetition. <strong>Do not copy</strong>: slow motion for emotion, music covering location sound, illustrative numbers as results. Borrow shots, not evidence.</p><h2>Three reusable moves</h2><p>1. Shoot the same action twice—failure and success—and cut them together; the viewer compares. 2. Every 15 seconds, give a hand/object close-up to ground abstract information. 3. Do not summarize at the end; leave an action or a question as a door to the next piece.</p><h2>Failure and check: breakdown is not copying</h2><p>The common failure is “the shots look similar, but the proof is fake.” Ask three questions: what happens if this beat is removed? Does the sound stand alone? Is the result checkable? If two answers fail, re-shoot or re-cut.</p><h2>Check</h2><ul><li>Labeled composite, not a real client.</li><li>Picture and sound serve the same job per beat.</li><li>At least one before/after is checkable.</li><li>Music does not cover key location sound.</li><li>Ending leaves an action/question, no summary.</li><li>Structure survives removing any one beat.</li></ul>
